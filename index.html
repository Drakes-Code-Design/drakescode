06 / FAQ

A few things clients usually ask.

Still deciding what your business needs? Start a conversation and we can sort through it together.

Ask a question ↘
How long does a website usually take?

Most small business websites take approximately one to three weeks. Larger custom projects may need more time depending on pages, content, and integrations.

Do I need a domain and hosting first?

No. I can explain what you need and guide you through choosing a domain and the right hosting option before launch.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website is built and tested for desktop computers, tablets, and modern mobile devices.

Can you update my website after launch?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance can cover content updates, technical support, minor design changes, backups, and general website care.

Can you connect booking or payments?

Yes. Third-party booking, ordering, scheduling, and payment platforms can often be integrated directly into your website.
